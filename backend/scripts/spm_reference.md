Bahasa Melayu 1103 (source: Sasbadi "Kertas Model SPM 1", publisher model paper; plus a search snippet for a second paper)
- Kertas 1 is 2h15m. Bahagian A: 30 markah, suggested 45 min, a VISUAL stimulus (pictures with captions) and the instruction
  "Huraikan pendapat anda tentang ...", length 200-250 patah perkataan (another source says 150-200).
- Bahagian B: choose ONE of 5 questions, "karangan lebih daripada 350 patah perkataan", shown as 100 markah on the model paper
  (suggested 1h30m). The 5 questions mix genres: opinion/langkah, syarahan (full speech text), huraian kebaikan, "Bincangkan.", and
  a story built on a peribahasa. Questions are short: a 1-2 sentence stimulus then ONE instruction word (Berikan pendapat, Tulis,
  Huraikan, Bincangkan). There are NO 3 bullet "isi pokok" hints on the real question.
- Kertas 2: 2h30m, 4 questions: Pemahaman (30), Rumusan, Tatabahasa (items marked [2]/[3]/[4] markah), KOMSAS sections with
  poem/short-story/novel questions at [6]/[7]/[8] markah. Language marking criteria: gramatis (kata dan ayat), kosa kata luas dan
  tepat, unsur bahasa bervariasi, ungkapan menarik, ejaan rumi betul. Holistic marking by band/criteria; a limited word count is marked.

Bahasa Inggeris 1119 (source: search snippets of trial-exam schemes; older format)
- Paper 1: Section A Directed Writing 35 marks (format 3 + content 12 + language 20; text types formal/informal letter, speech, report,
  article; about 45 min). Section B Continuous Writing 50 marks, choose 1 of 5 topics, about 1 hour. Types: descriptive, narrative,
  discursive, reflective. Band A is 44-50 and is judged on accuracy, varied sentence structure, wide precise vocabulary, accurate
  punctuation/spelling, well-planned linked paragraphs, consistent relevance, sustained reader interest.
- Continuous writing offers a CHOICE of 5 titles; it does not hand the candidate 3 guiding points.

Sejarah 1249 (source: search snippet about marking schemes)
- Kertas 2: Bahagian A structured, 40 marks, answer all 4; Bahagian B essay, 60 marks, answer 3 of 7. Marking is by points (isi/huraian)
  per Peraturan Pemarkahan, not by generic fluency bands.

Bahasa Cina (source: search snippet, low detail)
- Kertas 1 (writing) 100 marks, 1h45m: Bahagian A 30 marks, Bahagian B 70 marks. Word counts and the component split were NOT found.

Science (Fizik 4531 / Kimia 4541 / Biologi 4551) — source: Pandai exam-overview snippets, 2026-10-26 (current KSSM)
- Kertas 1: 40 objective items (multiple choice and combined choice), 40 marks.
- Kertas 2 (2h30m, 100 marks): Bahagian A = 8 structured questions, 60 marks, answer all, based on stimuli (graphs, tables, diagrams),
  ordered easy -> hard. Bahagian B = 2 questions, 20 marks, answer one. Bahagian C = 1 question, 20 marks (sources disagree on how many are offered).
- The split of marks between sub-parts of the B/C essays was NOT found; the app assumes parts summing to 20.
